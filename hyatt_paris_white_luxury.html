Our Story

About the Hotel

"A landmark of modern Parisian luxury, where the city's timeless spirit meets contemporary design at its most refined."

Rising 24 floors above the 17th arrondissement, Hyatt Regency Paris Étoile stands as one of Paris's most distinctive hotels. Located adjacent to the Palais des Congrès and moments from the Arc de Triomphe, we offer unrivalled access to both the city's cultural heart and its business district.

With 999 rooms and suites, two acclaimed dining venues, a rooftop bar, fitness centre, and comprehensive conference facilities — we have been a home away from home for world travellers since 1972.
